About

I grew up between Cyprus and New York, which gave me an instinct for how stories land across cultures. I studied journalism at Hunter College and had my first bylines in TV Guide Magazine before I graduated.

I work autonomously. I've streamlined my workflow so I can run a campaign independently from start to finish: briefing, recruiting creators, shooting, editing, publishing and reporting. When a step repeats, I build a tool for it with Claude Code, which is how I ran a 40+ creator program on my own. Years of teaching also made me good at turning complex ideas into simple ones, which is most of what great social content does.

Outside my day job, my sister and I run Girls in Beta, where we build AI automations for agencies and brands.
