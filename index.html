02AI in my work

How I use AI.

I use AI in two places: operations, where I build tools that replace manual work, and creative, where it speeds up ideation, scripting and production.

Operations
i.

A live creator dashboard

Every creator, post, view count and signup in one place, updated automatically, so leadership can check performance any time.

ii.

Scrapers and a weekly ranking

Post metrics come in from TikTok, Instagram and YouTube automatically, and creators are ranked by results every week.

iii.

Tracked links and auto reports

Every creator and ad gets its own UTM link, and a weekly report ties each one to signups and cost.
