another corridor just went live 👀 [Market A] → [Market B], settled in seconds on the XRP Ledger. what corridor do you want to see next? 👇

TikTok · 20s concept

Hook (0–2s): "How long does sending money abroad take? We timed it." Split screen: a traditional transfer timer vs. the ledger confirming. Close on the real settlement time. Caption sends people to the docs.

Same facts, same approval path. Ripple leads with the business outcome and stays measured. XRP leads with the community's own language and invites replies. Both pass through one calendar and one legal review, so the voices stay distinct without the facts drifting apart.

04

Real-time moments: respond, amplify or escalate

The framework I use when the timeline moves faster than the calendar, adapted for regulatory news and market volatility.

Green

Respond

Product questions, praise and confusion we can answer from published material. Reply within the hour, link the source.

Green

Amplify

Partner posts, developer wins and accurate community explainers. Quote, credit and thank people by name.

Amber

Hold

Price talk, rumors and anything that sounds like investment advice. Stay quiet, monitor sentiment and log it for Comms.

Red

Escalate

Regulatory or legal news, security claims, executive mentions. Pause scheduled posts, alert Comms, Legal and Policy, and publish only approved copy.
