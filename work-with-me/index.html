TikTok · 20s concept

Same facts, same approval path. The business version leads with the outcome and stays measured. The community version speaks the audience's own language and invites replies. One calendar and one review keep the voices distinct and the facts aligned.

04

Real-time moments: respond, amplify or escalate

The framework I use when the timeline moves faster than the calendar.

Green

Respond

Product questions, praise and confusion we can answer from published material. Reply within the hour, link the source.

Green

Amplify

Partner posts, customer wins and accurate community explainers. Quote, credit and thank people by name.

Amber

Hold

Rumors, pricing speculation and heated threads. Stay quiet, monitor sentiment and log it for Comms.

Red

Escalate

Legal or regulatory news, security claims, executive mentions. Pause scheduled posts, alert Comms and Legal, and publish only approved copy.

How I start in a new role

My first 90 days, adapted to each team in week one.

Days 1–30

Listen and audit

  • Audit every channel: content mix, cadence, engagement, sentiment
  • Map the approval path and agree fast-track rules for reactive posts
  • Write a one-page voice guide for each audience
  • Set up social listening for the brand, leaders and key topics
Days 31–60

Build the engine

  • Launch a shared editorial calendar tied to product and event dates
  • Stand up a creative pipeline with design, video and creators
  • Start a weekly report: reach, engagement, share of voice, conversions
  • Test two new formats per platform
Days 61–90

Scale what works

  • Run the first multi-week campaign around a major launch
  • Pilot a creator or community series
  • Build a drafting and coaching rhythm for leaders' accounts
  • Present a quarterly read-out with next quarter's bets
